# Day 10 Review Notes – Final Review and Exam Preparation

## 1. Purpose of Day 10

Day 10 is dedicated to final consolidation, light review, and mental preparation.  
No new material should be introduced.  
The goal is to reinforce confidence, ensure clarity on core concepts, and prepare for exam conditions.

Key goals:
- Review high-value artifacts.
- Revisit hardest questions.
- Finalize exam day logistics.
- Avoid cognitive overload.
- Enter the exam rested and focused.

---

## 2. Final Review of Core Artifacts

### Artifacts to Revisit
- Security Requirements Traceability Matrix (SRTM)
- Threat model (STRIDE)
- Data flow diagrams (DFDs)
- Architecture diagrams
- Secure coding examples
- Security test cases
- CI/CD security gates
- SBOM and dependency analysis
- Incident response playbook

### What to Check
- Traceability: Requirements → Design → Implementation → Testing → Deployment
- Completeness: No missing controls or phases
- Consistency: Terminology and concepts aligned across documents
- Accuracy: Threats and mitigations correctly mapped

---

## 3. Hardest Questions Review

### Steps
1. Identify 10–15 questions previously answered incorrectly or with low confidence.
2. Write down the correct reasoning in `hardest_questions.md`.
3. Identify the domain each question belongs to.
4. Summarize the root cause of confusion (conceptual gap, misinterpretation, lifecycle misunderstanding).
5. Revisit the relevant domain notes briefly.

### Common Hard Question Types
- Lifecycle phase identification
- Threat-to-mitigation mapping
- Requirements vs design vs implementation distinctions
- Supply chain and SBOM interpretation
- Governance and roles

---

## 4. Exam Day Checklist

### Knowledge Preparation
- Review high-level concepts only.
- Avoid deep dives or new topics.
- Skim notes for each domain.
- Revisit definitions and core principles.

### Logistics
- Ensure ID and exam confirmation details are ready.
- Know the exam location or testing platform requirements.
- Plan arrival time or testing environment setup.

### Mental Preparation
- Sleep adequately.
- Eat lightly before the exam.
- Avoid last-minute cramming.
- Focus on reasoning, not memorization.

---

## 5. Exam Strategy

### Time Management
- Do not rush; questions are scenario-based.
- Flag uncertain questions and revisit later.
- Avoid spending too long on any single item.

### Reasoning Approach
- Think like a security architect.
- Prioritize lifecycle alignment.
- Choose answers that emphasize governance, process, and traceability.
- Avoid overly technical interpretations unless clearly required.

### Common Pitfalls
- Overthinking technical details.
- Confusing implementation with design.
- Ignoring compliance or governance aspects.
- Misidentifying the correct SDLC phase.

---

## 6. Key Takeaways from Day 10

- Final review should be light and focused on confidence-building.
- The exam rewards structured reasoning and lifecycle thinking.
- Revisiting hardest questions strengthens weak areas without overwhelming.
- Proper rest and mental clarity are essential for optimal performance.
- Day 10 is about readiness, not learning.

---

## 7. Final Practice Notes

### Observations
- Mixed-domain questions require cross-domain understanding.
- Many items emphasize traceability and lifecycle alignment.
- Architecture and supply chain remain high-value domains.
- Requirements and testing questions often hinge on precise definitions.

### Final Adjustments
- Revisit any domain with persistent confusion.
- Review definitions for core concepts (CIA, STRIDE, SRTM, SBOM).
- Ensure understanding of governance roles and responsibilities.
